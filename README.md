# Open Teaching Resources

Open, editable and reusable teaching resources for **mathematics, data analytics
and computer science**.

This repository brings together teaching materials developed and used in my
teaching practice, from secondary education to higher education.

## About

I am a lecturer and mathematics teacher with experience in **applied
mathematics, statistics, Data Analytics and Data Science**.

I currently teach in higher education, particularly in business and engineering
schools, in both French and English.

→ [Academic portfolio and CV](Academic-Portfolio/README.md)

## Teaching resources

The repository is progressively organized by educational level and topic.

### Secondary education

- [Collège](College/) — mathematics exercises, revision sheets and solutions.
- [Lycée](Lycee/) — mathematics exercise sheets and solutions.

### Higher education

Resources in applied mathematics, statistics and Data Analytics will be added
progressively.

## Principles

The resources are designed to be:

- **open and editable** — source files are provided whenever possible;
- **reusable** — materials can be adapted to different teaching contexts;
- **progressive** — exercises move from direct application to reasoning and
  problem solving;
- **documented** — sources and design choices are recorded when relevant.

More information is available in the
[documentation](Documentation/README.md).

## Repository structure

- `Academic-Portfolio/` — academic CV and teaching portfolio;
- `College/` — collège mathematics resources;
- `Lycee/` — lycée mathematics resources;
- `Documentation/` — pedagogical and editorial principles;
- `Sources/` — references and source documentation;
- `Templates/` — reusable LaTeX templates.
